An AI-powered tool that generates custom coloring pages from text descriptions. Parents and teachers can create unique, printable line art tailored to any child's interests—from dinosaurs playing soccer to astronauts reading books.

Playlist Psychologist

An AI music personality analyzer that evaluates your Spotify playlists using a comprehensive psychological framework. It reveals patterns in your listening habits and provides insights into your mood, personality, and how your taste evolves.

Music is a window into our inner world. I've always been fascinated by what our listening choices reveal about us. This tool turns abstract music data into meaningful self-reflection and understanding.
