Immunally

Someone in my family lives with an autoimmune condition. For years we ran our own experiments, cut this, track that, wait and see, with no reliable way to tell what actually helped. We found some useful patterns and methods; really this is a data and experimentation problem. Right about the same time, building with AI became possible, so my husband and I built this together, for others to benefit from what we discovered. It tracks everything by voice, text or auto-pull from Apple Health, surfaces the underlying patterns, and lets you run self-experiments to see what works for your own body.

Every experiment on this page had the same friction: get stuck, screenshot the bug, drag it into the chat, lose the thread. Small enough to ignore, constant enough to grind. So I removed it: one command, no API key, nothing to configure by hand. I am not an engineer; what I could do was describe the problem precisely and keep insisting on simpler, which turned out to be the whole job. I still use it daily and it is a ton of fun to use what I built myself for my own itch.

Playlist Psychologist

I wanted to learn AI evals properly, not from a tutorial. That meant picking material I was personally invested in. Music is my emotional language, and most people's. It is also the perfect eval problem: nobody can tell you your reading is wrong, so plausible is worthless as a bar. The lesson was not the one I went in for. After a comprehensive eval with near-perfect scores, the app still handed people platitudes. What I learnt was evals are necessary. They are not a silver bullet.

2025

AI Coloring Page Generator

My son wanted a coloring page of a tsunami, a tornado and a volcano erupting together. It did not exist. AI was new then and I wanted to know whether you could ship something real for zero dollars: no API bill, no subscription, no hosting. You can. It is scrappy, I swapped the image backend twice when free tiers ran out, and he still uses it to generate absurd pages. It is the example I give anyone early in their career who asks how to start: go build something tangible. Cost and expertise is no longer a blocker.

2019

FAQ Plus for Microsoft Teams

I was an early PM on the Teams platform, and my job was to get developers building on it. Microsoft already had strong bot infrastructure and R&D, so I married that with what we had built in the Teams platform to offer support teams a way to be fast without sounding robotic. FAQ Plus answers what it knows from a knowledge base and hands everything else to a team of real experts, in the same Teams chat, without the person ever leaving Teams. I shipped it open source as a template so any team could take it and run. This was fun to do in pre-AI times, and it rapidly grew our platform usage.
